# Cutora Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 31, 2026**

**Effective Date: July 31, 2026**

Cutora ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is a photo cutout and editing application developed and operated by an individual developer. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App. It applies to all users worldwide, including users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, United States, Japan, South Korea, and Southeast Asian countries.

**By downloading, accessing, or using Cutora, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.**

---

## 1. Our Privacy Commitment: Local-First Architecture

Cutora is designed with a **privacy-by-design** approach. The App's core image processing functionality — including AI-powered cutout, background replacement, batch processing, and ID photo creation — operates entirely through an **on-device AI model**. This means:

- All images you import and edit are processed **exclusively on your device**;
- No images, photos, or editing data are ever uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on any remote server;
- We do **not** collect, store, or process any personal data about you on our servers;
- We do **not** employ any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or user-tracking technologies.

In essence, **we do not collect your personal data**. The information you create and edit within the App remains solely on your device under your control.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To be transparent and explicit, we do not collect:

- Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, government ID numbers);
- Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, advertising IDs);
- IP addresses or network location data;
- Geolocation or GPS data;
- Browsing history, usage analytics, or behavioral data;
- Contacts, calendar data, or health information;
- Biometric data or facial recognition templates (the on-device AI model processes pixel data for segmentation purposes only and does not create, store, or transmit any biometric identifiers);
- Clipboard contents or keystroke data.

---

## 3. Information Processed On-Device

The following data processing occurs locally on your device and never leaves your device:

- **Photo Library Access**: When you choose to import an image, the App requests permission to access your photo library. We access only the specific images you select. We do not scan, index, catalog, or transmit any other content in your library.
- **Camera Access**: When using the ID photo feature, the App may request camera access. Captured images are processed locally and are never transmitted.
- **On-Device Processing Data**: Intermediate computational data generated during AI model inference exists only in device memory (RAM) and is released immediately upon completion. It is never written to persistent storage or transmitted.

Cutora offers a premium membership subscription through Apple's App Store and StoreKit framework.

- All payment transactions are processed entirely by **Apple Inc.** We do not receive, access, or store any payment information (credit card numbers, Apple ID credentials, billing addresses).
- Apple's processing of your payment data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- We may receive limited subscription status information from Apple's Server-to-Server Notifications (e.g., whether a subscription is active, expired, or refunded). This information does not include payment details and is used solely to verify your entitlement to premium features.

## 5. Crash Reports (Optional)
